Keys

Click the keys, or use the keyboard: F1–F6 the menu, 0–9, Enter, Esc EXIT, Backspace, ↑ ↓, ← → as 4 and 6, ., - +/−, Shift (or s) the shift key, r or Space R/S. CHART and GLOBE: 4 6 turn, 8 2 tilt, 7 9 zoom, 5 fit; GLOBE / MERC on F4.

Routes

NAV47 reads ROUTE.TXT when it starts: the page restarts it.

Your position

The position and the clock zone go into NAV47.CFG, as SET on the calculator. The clock is this computer's; on the first start CLOCK TZ is set from it.

The calculator's disk

Kept in this browser (localStorage), never sent anywhere. PLAN: ENTER writes PLAN.CSV, shift ENTER PLAN.NMEA.
